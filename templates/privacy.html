Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect:

  • Account details, such as your name and email address.
  • Expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type, needed to keep the service running.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate Spendly: to show your expenses, produce summaries and category breakdowns, secure your account, and improve the service. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database managed by Spendly. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. We do not share your expense data with any third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your data deleted, contact us at support@spendly.example.
